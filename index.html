Davomat Dasturi

Kichik korxonalar uchun Windows dasturi: davomat, ish haqi, savdo, ombor va xarid hisobi.

Dastur nima qiladi

  • Xodimlar kartani tekkizib keladi va ketadi; dastur ish vaqtini va ish haqini hisoblaydi.
  • Savdo agentlari, omborchilar, kassirlar va xaridlar hisobini yuritadi.
  • Hisobotlarni Excel (.xlsx) fayliga chiqaradi.
  • Barcha ma'lumot shu kompyuterda saqlanadi. Ixtiyoriy ravishda rahbar o'z Google hisobini ulashi mumkin — shunda kunlik zaxira fayllari rahbarning o'z Google Drive'idagi "Davomat dasturi" papkasiga yuklanadi.

Google hisobi

Dastur Google'dan faqat uchta ruxsat so'raydi: hisob identifikatori (openid), email manzili (email) va Drive'da faqat dasturning o'zi yaratgan fayllar (drive.file). Dastur Drive'dagi boshqa fayllarni ko'rmaydi.

Maxfiylik siyosati

About (English)

Davomat Dasturi is a Windows desktop application for small businesses: employee attendance (card check-in/out), payroll, sales, warehouse and purchase accounting, with Excel export. All data is stored locally on the computer. Optionally, the business owner can connect their own Google account so that daily backup files are uploaded to a "Davomat dasturi" folder in their own Google Drive. The app requests only openid, email and drive.file and cannot see any other Drive files.
